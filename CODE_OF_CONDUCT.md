# Code of conduct

Worklings follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
That page is the full text and it applies here as written. This file is the short version in our own words.

## What we ask

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to Worklings, to Claude Code or to open source.
- Disagree about the work, not about the person. Give feedback that someone can act on, and take it gracefully.
- Say so when you get something wrong, and fix it.
- Keep what you share on topic and free of other people's private information.

## What we will not accept

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, trolling, and sexualised language or images.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Anything else that would be out of place in a professional setting.

## Where it applies

In this repository's issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews, and anywhere else someone speaks for the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Reports go to Zeus Studio, who maintain Worklings, at [support@zeusstudio.dev](mailto:support@zeusstudio.dev). You can
also use GitHub's "Report content" menu on a comment, issue or pull request, or open an issue titled "Conduct concern"
with no details in it, and the maintainer will arrange a private way to talk. Reports are read by the maintainer only and are kept private.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests, and may warn, temporarily block or permanently
block anyone whose conduct breaks these rules. The steps and their order follow the Contributor Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
